## What NOT to do on TikTok for this offer

- ❌ Don't promise returns. Ever. ("My clients made X%" = SEC-flavored trouble + attracts the wrong crowd)
- ❌ Don't use "I made $X" thumbnails or proof. Wrong audience signal — that's how beginners hook other beginners.
- ❌ Don't undersell the price. $4,500 is the filter. If you justify it too hard, you sound apologetic.
- ❌ Don't run a sale or countdown timer. The 5-spot scarcity is real; gimmick scarcity reads as fake.
- ❌ Don't put the Stripe link in the bio. Bio link goes to the **sales page** → application → discovery call → THEN Stripe. Filter aggressively.
- ❌ Don't reply to "is this a scam" comments. Pin one comment that says "Public dashboard at [URL] — verify before paying. RiskGuard is free standalone." Move on.
