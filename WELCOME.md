### ☐ 5. Start a trade journal — TODAY

For the next 12 weeks, every trade you take must be logged. Use this template (or copy to Notion/Google Sheets/whatever you prefer — Sheets recommended):

| Date/Time | Coin | Side | Setup | Entry | Stop | Target | Size $ | R:R | Result | Net $ | Notes (emotion, what you saw) |
|-----------|------|------|-------|-------|------|--------|--------|-----|--------|-------|-------------------------------|

**The Setup column is the most important.** Tag each trade with one of:
- `BREAKOUT` — playing a level break with momentum
- `MEAN_REVERT` — fading a stretched move back to value
- `TREND_PULLBACK` — buying a dip in an uptrend (or shorting a rally in a downtrend)
- `FUNDING` — collecting funding on extreme rates
- `NEWS` — reacting to a specific event
- `GUT` — honest tag for "I just felt like it"

I will read every entry weekly. The "Notes" column is where the gold lives — you'll be embarrassed reading it back in week 8 and that's the whole point.
