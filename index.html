Questions

The honest FAQ.

Will you guarantee profits?
No. Anyone who guarantees you profits in trading is lying or running an exit scam. What I guarantee is that in 90 days you will have a written system, working risk controls, and one bot live. The bot's profitability depends on whether the edge you bring me is real — and the first month of the program is dedicated to figuring that out before we automate anything.
What if I don't have a real edge?
That's the most common case and it's fine. We'll start with the closest HyperForge template (trend, mean-revert, breakout, funding) and parameterize it to fit your style and risk tolerance. You'll go live with a known-strategy bot you understand, instead of a black box. The "edge" in that case is process + risk discipline, not a magic setup.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. You'll work with HyperForge templates I've already built (the same ones running my own live bots — KronosBot, FibBot, EFCBot, SqueezeBot, TrendBot, etc.). You'll learn to configure them, not write them. If you want to learn to build bots from scratch, that's a different (future) cohort.
How much time per week?
Plan for 3–4 hours per week. 1 hour is our call, 30 minutes is the trade journal you keep, and 1.5–2 hours is reading, journaling, or applying the week's framework. If you can't commit that, the program won't work — don't sign up.
Why $4,500 instead of $499/month?
Because the outcome is fixed at 90 days. A monthly retainer creates the wrong incentive (drag it out forever). A flat 90-day price aligns us — you want the result, I want to deliver it and prove the program works for the next cohort. The $4,500 is paid in full at signup; no payment plans for the founding cohort.
What's RiskGuard and why is it included?
RiskGuard is a Telegram bot I built that connects to your Hyperliquid account and enforces your risk rules — it cancels orders that exceed your size limit, force-closes positions that hit your daily loss cap, and pings you on Telegram when something needs attention. It's free standalone (link), but configuring it correctly to your account, account size, and rules is part of the program.
Is the bot custom-built for me?
It's a HyperForge template parameterized to your edge — not a custom build from scratch. Custom dev exists as an optional upsell ($5–10k) for clients whose edge fits no existing template, but ~90% of edges fit one of the 7 templates I already run live with my own money. Same code I trust with my own account.
What if I want to drop out?
Pro-rated refund within the first 14 days, no questions. After day 14, no refunds — by then I've already done the trade journal review and risk audit, which is most of the upfront work. If you finish the 90 days and feel you didn't get what was promised, I'll do another 30 days at no cost to make it right.
